## My Favorite Movie Series: Harry Potter

My all-time favorite movie series is **Harry Potter**. Following the journey of Harry, Ron, and Hermione at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry never gets old.

### Why It's My Favorite
* **World-Building:** The Wizarding World feels immersive, detailed, and magical from start to finish.
* **Character Growth:** Watching the characters grow from kids into young adults facing real stakes makes the story deeply engaging.
* **Replay Value:** Every film brings a distinct tone—from lighthearted wonder in the early movies to dark, intense storytelling in the finale.

### The 8 Films
1. *Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone* (2001)
2. *Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets* (2002)
3. *Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban* (2004)
4. *Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire* (2005)
5. *Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix* (2007)
6. *Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince* (2009)
7. *Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 1* (2010)
8. *Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 2* (2011)
